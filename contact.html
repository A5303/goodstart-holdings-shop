Frequently Asked Questions

What are your delivery charges?

Standard delivery is KSh 200 within Nairobi, free for orders over KSh 2,000. Express same-day delivery is KSh 400, free for orders over KSh 3,000.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery: 1-2 business days. Express delivery: Same day if ordered before 2 PM. Outside Nairobi: 2-3 business days.

Do you offer wholesale prices?

Yes, we offer competitive wholesale pricing for businesses, institutions, and bulk purchases. Contact our bulk sales team for customized quotes.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 7-day return policy for unused items in original packaging. We provide free pickup for returns within our delivery areas.
